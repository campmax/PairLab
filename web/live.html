How to read this
Z-score measures deviation from a historical relationship, not how many dollars a stock is truly underpriced. A negative candidate suggests examining long A / short B; positive suggests short A / long B. Failed training screens produce no candidate.
Daily-history models are recalculated as data arrives. Intraday scores are provisional: the original strategy used completed closing signals and later opening fills. The optional intraday paper experiment is a different execution study and has no proven profitability.
Quotes older than 120 seconds, timestamps more than 30 seconds apart, closed sessions and provider failures block current candidates. Public-feed delays and interruptions can occur. No account or broker API key is needed.
