The research question

Can a statistically screened relationship between two stocks generate returns after trading costs and the expense of short selling?

01

Fit using earlier observations

log(TRI_A) = intercept + beta × log(TRI_B) + spread

TRI is a forward-built total-return index. It accounts for each split and dividend when that event occurs. The spread is the unexplained difference between the two paths.

02

Reject weak or unstable relationships

Apply an Engle–Granger residual test with a fixed lag, the two-series 1% critical value, a positive hedge-ratio range, a half-life filter and a hedge-ratio stability filter. This is a screening rule, not proof that the relationship will persist. The method assumes appropriate nonstationary input series; no separate integration-order gate is implemented.

03

Trade an unusual spread

When the spread is low, buy A and short B. When it is high, short A and buy B. Dollar allocations follow 1 : beta; that does not guarantee dollar neutrality or market-beta neutrality. Both legs are assumed executable together.

04

Account for the whole position

Short-sale proceeds increase cash but create an equal liability. They do not count as profit. Short collateral is excluded from free cash when charging financing. Dividends accrue on the ex-date as a payment-date approximation.

05

Move the window forward

At each scheduled boundary, close old positions at the open and fit the next model from preceding closes. Start generating new signals after that day’s close. Cancel unfilled orders at a boundary. Liquidate at the final scheduled open so the ledger reconciles to cash.

What this experiment cannot establish

  • The stock list consists of current surviving companies chosen retrospectively. It is not a point-in-time investable universe.
  • At most four pairs are tested per window at 1% each. The Bonferroni bound is 4% for those tests under their assumptions; repeated testing across time and strategy research is not controlled.
  • Borrow availability is assumed. Actual historical borrow rates, recalls, locates, intraday margin calls, liquidity limits, taxes and execution failures are not modeled.
  • Daily stops execute at a later open. Overnight gaps can exceed the intended loss limit. Modeled maintenance is a simplified capital rule, not a broker’s full margin schedule.
  • Price data can be revised or contain missing corporate actions. The importer reconstructs historical share units from reported later splits; this is not a point-in-time vendor archive.
  • Higher Sharpe or a significant training test does not establish a live trading edge. There is no parameter optimizer in this release.
